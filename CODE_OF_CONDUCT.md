# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to play music together. Behave here the
way you would in a rehearsal room with people you respect.

## What is expected

Be civil and assume the other person is trying to help. Critique the code and
the design, not the person who wrote it. Take a correction well, and give one
without an audience where you can. Remember that most people here are doing
this in their spare time.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind. Insults, demeaning remarks, or comments about someone's
identity, background, or ability. Sexual attention or imagery. Publishing
somebody's private information. Deliberately derailing discussions or wearing
people down.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits, and anywhere else the project
is being discussed, including in private contact that starts here.

## Reporting

Email seanreid.mail@gmail.com. Reports stay between you and me. I will say
what I decide and why.

I maintain this project on my own, so I am also the one who enforces this. If
the problem is my own behaviour, say so plainly and I will take it seriously.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened: a private word, a public correction, edited or
removed comments, or a block from the repository. I would rather resolve
something with a conversation than a ban, but the comfort of people acting in
good faith comes first.
